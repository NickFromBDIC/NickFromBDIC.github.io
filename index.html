Medical imaging and machine learning

Radiomics &
feature fusion.

I’m Yuan, a PhD researcher at University College Dublin. I work on feature fusion for medical imaging, particularly combining radiomics with learned image representations.

My current work focuses on CT-based renal cell carcinoma classification. I investigate how handcrafted radiomic features complement 2D and 3D pretrained representations, and evaluate fusion strategies across datasets.

Explore my publications ↓Background & experience

Research focus

My research explores how radiomics and feature fusion can support clinical assessment from medical images. In renal cancer, I combine quantitative tumour features with learned image representations to study whether CT can better distinguish tumour subtypes before surgery. The broader aim is to support non-invasive tumour characterisation and provide useful imaging evidence for clinical decision-making.
